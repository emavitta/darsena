## Prompt iniziale

Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: refined panoramic welcome illustration for Darsena, a macOS developer tool named after the urban harbor in Milan.
Primary request: A sophisticated original editorial illustration of Milan's Darsena at blue hour, grounded in the actual low-rise urban character around Porta Ticinese, rather than a generic seaport. Seen from a slightly elevated pedestrian viewpoint on the quay across the long harbor basin. Wide horizontal composition, roughly 2:1. The lower half is a calm deep inky blue/green expanse of water. One warm pale limestone quay cuts a confident diagonal across the immediate foreground. Across the far bank, a restrained row of recognizably Milanese low ochre, chalk, pale gray and muted brick stucco palazzi with dark shutters and simple tile roofs; minimal architectural strokes. Quiet broad quay steps descend toward the water. A slender low contemporary urban footbridge spans the canal mouth toward the left edge, kept small and believable. The basin is the main subject; architecture is a continuous low backdrop, no dominant landmark. Large soft ivory dusk sky with a delicate cool gradient, faint warm windows just beginning to illuminate. Water carries a few broad, carefully placed warm reflections and clean horizontal ripples.
Style/medium: high-end Italian editorial illustration, deliberate sharp silhouettes, architectural clarity, elegant flattened depth and restrained matte color fields; subtle painterly shading within large forms, beautifully controlled edges. Design-conscious, calm, grown-up. NOT photorealism, NOT a 3D render, NOT a stock vector illustration with repetitive window grids. Geometry feels observed, not mechanically perfect. Composition should remain beautifully legible when used as a 560px-wide app illustration. Left and right edges compositionally quiet so cropping is flexible.
Palette: ink navy/blue-green water, graphite details, warm pale stone, chalk-ivory sky, muted ochre and terracotta buildings. Overall limited sophisticated palette, water dominates. No saturated orange sunset, no purple, no neon. Warm/cool material contrast connects the illustration to a charcoal stone and pale limestone app icon.
Constraints: no people, no boats, no ships, no anchors, no logos, no text, no captions, no letters, no map pin, no pasted-in Duomo cathedral, no mountains, no grand tourist landmark, no skyline towers, no cranes, no round badge, no framing tile, no isometric miniature, no cartoon outlines. Fill the complete rectangular image; no outside border or transparency.
Output: a single panoramic illustration at 1536x768.
